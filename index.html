Common questions

About UzaPay POS.

What is UzaPay?

UzaPay is an offline-first point of sale system for mobile and desktop businesses. It is designed to help businesses manage sales, inventory, receipts, staff activity and reporting in one workflow.

Does UzaPay work without internet?

UzaPay is designed around an offline-first sales workflow, so core sales activity can continue when connectivity drops. Changes can synchronize when the device reconnects.

Who is UzaPay for?

UzaPay is being designed for Kenyan retail shops, wholesalers, service businesses and mobile sellers that need practical point-of-sale tools.

Can I use UzaPay on mobile and desktop?

Yes. UzaPay is being built for mobile and desktop use so businesses can keep a consistent sales workflow across the devices their teams already use.
