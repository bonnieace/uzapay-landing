POS software in Kenya

Run the sale, then run the business.

UzaPay brings checkout, stock control, receipts, staff access and business reporting into one offline-first POS workflow. The goal is simple: make everyday operations easier to manage whether the business is behind a counter, across a shop floor or moving between locations.

For a Kenyan business, a POS system has to fit real operating conditions. UzaPay is being designed so core sales activity can continue during connectivity interruptions and synchronize when a connection becomes available again.

Choosing a POS system

Look beyond the till.

A POS system affects more than the checkout screen. It shapes how products are added, how staff interact with the system, how receipts are handled, how stock changes are tracked and how a business reviews its performance.

UzaPay is being built around those connected workflows, with an emphasis on simplicity for staff and visibility for the business owner.
