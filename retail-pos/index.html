Retail POS in Kenya

Make the counter the easy part of the day.

Retail teams need to move quickly without losing track of products, transactions or staff activity. UzaPay combines point-of-sale checkout with the operational tools around it, so the till does not become a separate system from the shop itself.

From a neighbourhood shop to a growing multi-device retail operation, the workflow is designed around repeatable daily tasks: find products, sell, record the transaction, keep stock visible and review the business.
