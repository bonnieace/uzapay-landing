Offline point of sale

Your sales should not depend on a perfect connection.

Internet reliability can vary from one business location to the next. UzaPay is designed around the assumption that connectivity can disappear at the wrong time.

The core idea is straightforward: keep the important sales workflow available locally, then synchronize business data when the device reconnects. That gives staff a clearer fallback than simply waiting for the network to come back.

Work locally. Sync when connected.
How it works
1Sell locally when connectivity is unavailable.
2Save transaction activity safely on the device.
3Reconnect and synchronize outstanding changes.
4Continue from the same business workflow.
Built around continuity

Offline is a product decision, not a patch.

UzaPay treats offline operation as part of the POS experience rather than as an emergency mode added after the fact. The result is intended to feel like the same product whether the connection is strong, weak or temporarily unavailable.
